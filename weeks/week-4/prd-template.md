# PRD headings

Tap the copy icon at the top right of the grey box. Paste it into your notes, fill in every heading in your own words, then paste it into a new file called `PRD.md` in your repo.

```
# [Project name]
By: @[your GitHub names] · Date:

## The problem
## Who it's for (persona)
## User story
As ___, I want to ___, so that ___.
## The one thing it does
## How we'll know it works
It works when ___ can ___ without help.
## Phone or computer
## My designs
(one line per design: what it shows, what each tap does)
## My colors
60%: · 30%: · 10%:
## Must-have / nice-to-have
Must-haves are done by code freeze, Thu 10 Dec.
## What it won't do
## Apps that inspire me
## AI tool
```

**Extra:** show your photos inside PRD.md. Tap a photo in your repo to see its name, then type it under My designs like this: `![Design 1](IMG_1234.jpg)`
