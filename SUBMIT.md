# 🚀 Pro builders: get your project in

You already code, so you skip the weekly assignments. Build wherever you like: your own GitHub account, public or private. Everything we need from you goes on your 🚀 Pro project issue, as comments.

**Deadline: Thu 10 Dec, 8 pm** (code freeze, same as everyone).

## 1. Send us your idea by Sun 1 Nov, 8 pm

**[Open the Pro project form](https://github.com/hechoenosa/project-template/issues/new?template=pro.yml)** (you need to be signed in to GitHub). In the title, write your project name ending in `-pro`, like `volt-pro`. Then tell us who is on your team, what your idea is, who it's for, whether you started it before Hecho en Osa, and what you want to build by Thu 10 Dec. It takes about 10 minutes, and you don't need to have built anything yet.

This becomes your 🚀 Pro project issue. Within 2 days a mentor answers there.

> Your issue is public. Keep it short and put the details in your PRD if you'd rather not share them.

## 2. Post your PRD and TRD by Sun 8 Nov, 8 pm

Comment on your issue with both. Copy [PRD.md](https://github.com/hechoenosa/project-template/blob/main/PRD.md) and [TRD.md](https://github.com/hechoenosa/project-template/blob/main/TRD.md) as a starting point:

- **PRD**: What your product does, who it's for, and the one thing that has to work on Demo Day.
- **TRD**: The tech stack you're using or plan to use, and how the parts fit together.

If your repo is public, you can link the files instead. A mentor reads them and replies.

## 3. Build

Your repo, your tools, your pace. Starting fresh? Click **Use this template** on [project-template](https://github.com/hechoenosa/project-template) and you get the README, PRD and TRD ready to fill in.

- **Private repo?** To get feedback on your code, add your mentor and `@nastyasimpson` as collaborators (Settings → Collaborators).
- **Updates:** Every couple of weeks, comment on your issue with what's done, what's next and where you're stuck.
- **Help:** Come to any Saturday Build Hour or workshop you like.

## 4. Submit by Thu 10 Dec, 8 pm

Comment on your issue with:

1. **✅ Ready**
2. **A demo video**, 2 to 3 minutes, showing your app working. A screen recording is best. An unlisted YouTube or Google Drive link is fine.
3. **How to try it**: A live link, or screenshots if it isn't online.
4. **Your repo link**, if you want to share it.
5. **If you started before Hecho en Osa**: What you built during the program.

A mentor replies to confirm you're in and sends Demo Day details.

## 5. Demo Day

You present your project live, with slides: the problem, a demo and what you built. Judges see your demo video and your presentation.

## Rules that still apply

- **Stay private:** Handles or first names only, no faces, phone numbers or keys in your code or your video.
- **Explain every line.** AI is allowed. Code nobody on your team can explain isn't.
