# Week 4 · Your TRD + first AI tools (Lee, Anastasia, Zamir)

**Build Hour:** Sat 14 Nov · **Leads:** Lee (TRD), Anastasia (data), Zamir (what AI is, ethics) · **Due:** Sun 15 Nov, 8 pm · **Phase:** Develop · **Checkpoint 2**

The PRD says *what* and *for whom*. The TRD says *how*. By Sunday your team knows every screen, every piece of data, which tools you'll use and how you'll test that it works. This is also the week AI joins the team. You set up Copilot and use it for the first time, to question your TRD, not to write it.

> **🎯 Your mission this week**
>
> 1. Turn your paper screens into a screen list.
> 2. Decide your data and your level.
> 3. Write `TRD.md` sections 1–8 together, by hand.
> 4. Set up Copilot and ask it what's missing from your TRD.
> 5. Open one issue per screen and your first pull request.
>
> **Due:** Sun 15 Nov, 8 pm · **Post on:** your team issue
