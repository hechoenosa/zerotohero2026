# Week 5 · Assignment

## STEP 1 · Watch the video 🎬

"Anatomy of a prompt" (Anastasia), about 8 minutes.

## STEP 2 · Write PROMPT.md 🧩

Open `PROMPT.md`. Each part tells you which PRD or TRD section to copy from:

1. Who you are ("We're 3 builders, ages 12–14, in Ojochal")
2. Who it's for → PRD 1
3. The problem → PRD 2
4. The ONE thing → PRD 4
5. Step by step → PRD 5
6. Screens → TRD 2
7. Level and tools → TRD 4
8. Files → TRD 5
9. Done when → TRD 6, and "Don't build" → PRD 7

Commit: `Add our prompt`. Due **Fri 20 Nov, 8 pm**.

## STEP 3 · At the Vibe Jam 🎉

1. Pick one screen issue, e.g. `#3 Screen: Start`.
2. `git checkout -b start-screen`
3. Paste parts 1–9 into Copilot chat, then: `Build only the Start screen from part 6.`
4. Read every line it writes. Ask it: `Explain this line like I'm 12.`
5. Save, open in the browser, check your tests from TRD 6.
6. Commit, push, open a pull request. In the description write `Fixes #3`.
7. Mentor reviews → **Merge**.

The Week 4 AI rules still apply.

## 🏫 No computer at home?

The Vibe Jam has laptops. Write your part of `PROMPT.md` on paper and bring it.

## ✅ Done when

- [ ] `PROMPT.md` in the repo by Friday
- [ ] One screen merged with `Fixes #n`

## 🆘 Stuck?

Check **[STUCK.md](../../STUCK.md)** first. Still stuck after 10 minutes? Ask, with a screenshot, what you tried and what you expected.

¡Hecho en Osa! 🌴
