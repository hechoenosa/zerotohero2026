# 🚀 Pro builders: get your project in

You already code, so you skip the weekly assignments. Your project lives in the Hecho en Osa org, in a repo made from [project-template](https://github.com/hechoenosa/project-template), just like every Zero to Hero team.

**Deadline: Thu 10 Dec, 8 pm** (code freeze, same as everyone).

## 1. Ask for your repo

Go to [Issues → New issue](https://github.com/hechoenosa/zerotohero2026/issues/new/choose) → **🚀 Pro project**. Fill in the repo name, your team's GitHub handles and one sentence. One person per team.

Within 2 days you get an invite to `hechoenosa/your-project`, already set up from project-template. Ask early, and no later than **Thu 3 Dec**.

## 2. Build

Your way, your tools, your pace. Come to any Saturday Build Hour or workshop if you want help. Nobody checks your weekly progress.

## 3. Fill in the README

The README in your repo is the template. Fill in **sections 1–9** in your own words: what it is, who made it, how to try it, how the code works, tools, libraries, what works, who tested it, and your final presentation at `presentation/final.pdf`.

## 4. Submit

By **Thu 10 Dec, 8 pm**:
1. Tick every box in the **✅ In-hack checklist** at the bottom of your README.
2. Push.
3. Comment **✅ Ready** on your 🚀 Pro project issue.

A mentor replies to confirm you're in and sends Demo Day details.

## Rules that still apply

- **Stay private:** handles or first names only, your noreply email, no faces, phone numbers or keys in your code.
- **Explain every line.** AI is allowed. Code nobody on your team can explain isn't.

All rules: [RULES.md](RULES.md) · Questions: the Hackathon WhatsApp group
